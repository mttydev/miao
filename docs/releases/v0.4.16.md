# miao v0.4.16

[简体中文](https://github.com/mttydev/miao/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.4.16.zh.md)

v0.4.16 makes the sidebar's context bar readable. The bar 0.4.15 added drew its
filled share and its empty rest in one colour, so every reading looked like a
full window, and it rounded a small share down to no cells at all.

## The context bar says how full the window is

The row states a fraction — `context 105.0k of 967.2k · 10%` — and the bar
beside it exists to answer "how close is compaction" without arithmetic. Two
defects stopped it answering:

- **One ink for both runs.** The filled share and the empty rest were pushed as
  a single span in one colour. Every reading therefore painted a solid block,
  and a tenth of the window looked exactly like a full one.
- **Small shares rounded to nothing.** The filled cells were integer-divided,
  and the bar never shrinks below six cells, so on a narrow sidebar every
  reading below seventeen percent filled zero cells: the bar sat empty while the
  figure beside it said a tenth of the window was in use.

The two runs now carry different ink — the share in the accent tone, or the
warning tone at and above eighty percent, with the empty rest in the theme's
quieter element tone — and any non-zero occupancy fills at least one cell. The
bar now moves with the percentage it sits beside, so reading it is faster than
reading the figure.

An unknown ceiling still paints no bar at all, because an unmeasured window is
not a full one.

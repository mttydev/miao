# miao v0.3.83

[简体中文](https://github.com/oxdingzg/miao/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.3.83.zh.md)

v0.3.83 keeps a reader in place: a transcript scrolled into history no longer jumps back to the newest rows when a background projection lands.

## Reading history stays put

The engine projects only the newest window of a Session. When that projection arrived while the transcript was scrolled into history, it replaced the pages the reader had paged in and clamped the viewport to the window's end — the newest rows. Every background projection, whether a committed step, a transport resync or the quiet-frame recovery, pulled the view back to the bottom.

A detached transcript now keeps the rows it has loaded and holds the projection until the reader returns to the tail with `End` or by scrolling to the bottom. The composer and status rows keep updating while history is held.

# miao v0.5.9

[简体中文](https://github.com/mttydev/miao/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.5.9.zh.md)

v0.5.9 is a patch for a stray character that could survive a scroll.

## What a scroll leaves behind

After a few scrolls, a glyph from an older frame could stay at the right edge of
a row. The rows that moved were repainted, but a cell the terminal dropped was
never sent again: ratatui's diff compares against its own previous buffer, which
still believes that cell is drawn. The report came from Windows, where the
console re-encodes the screen it hands the terminal and can lose a cell while
re-encoding.

## One repaint after the scroll settles

Every viewport move now schedules a single full repaint 200 ms after the last
one: the wheel, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End, the single-line nudges, switching
Sessions, and the jump back to the tail when a prompt is submitted. The repaint
empties both of ratatui's diff buffers instead of clearing the screen, so every
cell is sent again without a blank flash; a burst of notches costs one full
frame at the end instead of one per notch.
